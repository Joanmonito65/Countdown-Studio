A little moment of help

Choose a name, pick the exact date and time, then make the countdown your own. Your preview updates as you edit. Save it to keep it in your list on this device.

Time zones: Normally, your selected time zone defines one exact worldwide moment. Turn on Use each viewer’s local time to make the countdown reach zero at the same date and clock time in each person’s own time zone instead. This setting is off by default and is saved with the countdown.

Sharing: Choose Share Fullscreen to open the presentation view, or Share Edit Mode to open the studio with the countdown loaded. The website must be hosted online for other people to open the link. Browsers require the viewer to tap the Fullscreen control to enter true browser fullscreen. Export downloads your saved countdowns as a JSON backup.
